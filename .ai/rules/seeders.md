# Seeders

## hr@example.com is the agency's real HR account, not a fixture
The agency decided HR access lives on one shared account with no employee record attached, and chose to keep the seeded hr@example.com as that account. It is used by more than one HR staff member.

Consequences to keep in mind:
- Treat it as production data. Do not delete it, and do not assume its password is still "password" — the seeder only repairs the role on an account that already exists, so a re-run will not reset it. Deleting it and re-seeding WOULD recreate it with the factory password.
- Everything done there is attributed to "HR Officer", so submitted_by / approver_user_id / a report's "Generated by" cannot identify which person acted. If per-person attribution is ever needed (the planned export audit log is the likely trigger), that is the point to move to one named HR account per officer.
- Approval queues are built from head designation on an employee, never from the role, so this account has an empty approvals queue and an empty notification bell by design. Both belong to the person's own employee-linked account.
